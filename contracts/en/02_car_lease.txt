VEHICLE LEASING AGREEMENT — PRIVATE USE
Form LEA-P/2026 — Particular and general conditions

A. PARTICULAR CONDITIONS

A.1 Vehicle: passenger car, list price on the road CHF 28'000.00
A.2 Down payment (increased first instalment) on delivery: CHF 4'000.00
A.3 Monthly instalment: CHF 349.00, due on the 1st day of each month
A.4 Term of the agreement: 48 (forty-eight) months
A.5 Residual value / purchase option price at expiry: CHF 9'800.00
A.6 One-off contract fee: CHF 390.00, due on signature
A.7 Maximum mileage allowed: 15'000 km per contract year
A.8 Excess mileage: CHF 0.25 for each kilometre in excess
A.9 Unused mileage: reimbursed at CHF 0.10 per kilometre, up to a maximum
    of 5'000 km in total

B. GENERAL CONDITIONS

B.1 Ownership
The vehicle remains the property of the Leasing Company for the entire term of
the agreement. The Client acquires the right of use only.

B.2 Insurance
B.2.1 The Client is required to take out and maintain, for the entire term of
      the agreement, fully comprehensive insurance cover with a deductible not
      exceeding CHF 1'000.00. The cover is not included in the instalment.
B.2.2 Failure to take out or maintain the cover constitutes a material breach
      and entitles the Leasing Company to immediate termination.

B.3 Maintenance
Routine and extraordinary maintenance, road taxes and running costs are borne
in full by the Client and are not included in the instalment.

B.4 Early termination at the Client's request
B.4.1 The Client may request early termination of the agreement after at least
      12 months from commencement, subject to written notice of 60 (sixty) days.
B.4.2 In that case the Client is required to pay the sum of the instalments not
      yet fallen due, increased by an indemnity of 5.00% of that same sum in
      respect of unamortised value.
B.4.3 The vehicle inspection and return fee of CHF 450.00 is also due.
B.4.4 Any excess mileage is calculated as at the date of return.

B.5 Purchase option
B.5.1 At natural expiry the Client may purchase the vehicle at the price set out
      in point A.5, by giving 30 days' notice.
B.5.2 Absent such notice, the vehicle is returned to the Leasing Company.

B.6 Return
B.6.1 On return the vehicle is inspected. Damage beyond normal wear and tear is
      charged to the Client according to the attached damage schedule.
B.6.2 Inspection fee at natural expiry: CHF 250.00.

B.7 Term and renewal
The agreement does not renew automatically and terminates at the expiry set out
in point A.4, save for exercise of the purchase option.

B.8 Late payment
Default interest of 5.00% per annum on overdue amounts; reminder fee of
CHF 30.00 per notice.
