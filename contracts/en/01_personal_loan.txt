CONSUMER CREDIT AGREEMENT
Unsecured personal loan — Form PL/2026

Summary of terms and financial conditions

Art. 1 — Subject
The Lender grants the Client an unsecured personal loan with a net disbursed
amount of CHF 12'000.00 (twelve thousand/00), repayable according to the
amortisation schedule attached hereto.

Art. 2 — Financial conditions
2.1 Total amount of credit: CHF 12'000.00
2.2 Repayment term: 36 (thirty-six) months
2.3 Amount of each monthly instalment: CHF 375.50
2.4 Fixed nominal annual rate (NAR): 7.90%
2.5 Annual percentage rate of charge (APR): 10.34%
2.6 First instalment falls due on the 5th day of the month following disbursement.

Art. 3 — Fees and charges
3.1 File handling fee: CHF 250.00, payable at the time of disbursement.
3.2 Collection and servicing fee on each instalment: CHF 3.00 per instalment,
    charged together with the instalment itself.
3.3 Periodic statements: free of charge if delivered electronically.
3.4 Stamp duty on the annual statement: borne by the Client where applicable.

Art. 4 — Insurance cover
4.1 The Client may, but is not obliged to, join the group policy
    "Credit Protection" covering the risks of death, permanent disability
    and loss of employment.
4.2 Where the Client joins, the premium is CHF 8.50 for each instalment and
    is charged together with the instalment.
4.3 Declining to join does not affect the financial conditions set out in Art. 2.

Art. 5 — Early repayment
5.1 The Client may repay the outstanding capital early, in whole or in part,
    at any time, subject to written notice of 30 (thirty) days.
5.2 On early repayment a charge of 1.00% of the outstanding capital repaid
    early is due, where the remaining term of the agreement exceeds 12 months.
5.3 Where the remaining term is 12 months or less, no charge is due.
5.4 The fees under Art. 3.2 accrued up to the date of settlement remain due.

Art. 6 — Late payment
6.1 Where an instalment is paid late, default interest is due at the nominal
    annual rate increased by 2 percentage points.
6.2 Written reminder fee: CHF 20.00 per reminder.

Art. 7 — Term and renewal
7.1 The agreement terminates upon payment of the final instalment.
7.2 This agreement does not provide for automatic renewal.

Art. 8 — Right of withdrawal
8.1 The Client may withdraw from the agreement within 14 (fourteen) days of
    conclusion, without giving reasons and without penalty, by written notice.
8.2 On withdrawal the Client returns the capital disbursed together with the
    interest accrued up to the date of return.

Art. 9 — Place of jurisdiction
Any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the Client's domicile.
