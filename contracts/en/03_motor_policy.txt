MOTOR THIRD PARTY LIABILITY AND MOBILITY INSURANCE POLICY
Insurance conditions — 2026 edition

1. SUBJECT AND TERM

1.1 This policy covers third party liability arising from the use of the vehicle
    identified in the policy schedule, together with any additional cover taken
    out.

1.2 Term of the agreement: 1 (one) year, commencing on the date stated on the
    schedule.

1.3 RENEWAL. At expiry the agreement renews automatically from year to year,
    unless cancelled by either party.

1.4 CANCELLATION. Notice of cancellation must reach the Company in writing at
    least 90 (ninety) days before the annual expiry date. Notice received after
    that deadline is ineffective and the agreement is deemed renewed for a
    further year.

2. PREMIUM

2.1 Gross annual premium: CHF 780.00, inclusive of taxes and duties.

2.2 Instalments. On request the premium may be paid in monthly instalments. In
    that case the monthly instalment is CHF 68.00 and the total annual premium
    amounts to CHF 816.00.

2.3 The additional charge under point 2.2 is the consideration for payment by
    instalments and is not refundable on early termination.

2.4 Issue and receipt fee: CHF 25.00, one-off, on conclusion.

3. MULTI-YEAR COMMITMENT DISCOUNT

3.1 A Policyholder who undertakes to keep the policy in force for 3 (three)
    consecutive annual periods receives a discount of 15.00% on the gross annual
    premium under point 2.1.

3.2 On cancellation or termination before completion of the third annual period,
    the Policyholder is required to repay in full the discounts received in the
    preceding annual periods.

3.3 Repayment falls due within 30 days of the Company's demand.

4. DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS

4.1 Deductible for an at-fault claim, collision cover: CHF 1'000.00.
4.2 Third party liability limit of indemnity: CHF 100'000'000.00.
4.3 Theft and fire cover: deductible CHF 500.00.

5. PREMIUM ADJUSTMENTS

5.1 The Company may adjust the premium with effect from the annual expiry date,
    by giving the Policyholder notice at least 25 days before that date.
5.2 In that case the Policyholder may cancel the agreement within 30 days of
    receiving the notice, by way of derogation from the deadline in point 1.4.

6. CLAIMS

6.1 Claims must be notified within 5 days of becoming aware of the event.
6.2 The bonus-malus system provides for a downgrade of 3 levels for each
    at-fault claim settled.

7. JURISDICTION

Any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the Policyholder's domicile.
